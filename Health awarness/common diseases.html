Common Diseases and Health Info

Malaria

Symptoms:

  • Fever and chills
  • Headache
  • Muscle pain
  • Nausea and vomiting

Prevention:

  • Sleep under insecticide-treated mosquito nets
  • Use mosquito repellent
  • Eliminate standing water near your home

When to See a Doctor:

  • If you develop fever after being in a malaria-prone area

Flu (Influenza)

Symptoms:

  • Fever and chills
  • Cough and sore throat
  • Body aches
  • Fatigue

Prevention:

  • Get vaccinated annually
  • Wash hands regularly
  • Avoid close contact with sick people

When to See a Doctor:

  • If symptoms worsen or last more than a week
  • If you're in a high-risk group (elderly, pregnant, etc.)

Diabetes

Symptoms:

  • Frequent urination
  • Increased thirst and hunger
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Fatigue and blurred vision

Prevention:

  • Eat a healthy diet with whole grains, vegetables, and lean protein
  • Avoid sugary drinks
  • Exercise regularly (at least 30 minutes a day)
  • Maintain a healthy weight
  • Get regular medical check-ups to monitor blood sugar
  • Avoid smoking
  • Stay active and avoid sitting for long periods
