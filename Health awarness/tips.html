Health Tips for a Better Life

Daily Health Tips

  • Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily
  • Take a 30-minute walk or do light exercise
  • Sleep 7–9 hours each night
  • Take short breaks while working
  • Practice deep breathing or meditation

Healthy Food Suggestions

  • Eat more fruits and vegetables
  • Choose whole grains over refined ones
  • Limit processed sugar and junk food
  • Include lean protein like fish or beans
  • Use healthy fats like olive oil
